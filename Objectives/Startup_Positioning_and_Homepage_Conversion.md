## Objective

Reposition the ClientFirst Labs website to target **early-stage Canadian startups** and increase **qualified founder meetings**.

---

# Business Context

ClientFirst Labs is a **software development agency** currently positioned as a **general custom software development company**.

This positioning attracts:

* Small businesses
* Low-budget clients
* Non-startup work

This reduces:

* Qualified founder meetings
* Contract size
* Sales efficiency

---

## Target Market

Primary Audience:

Early-stage Canadian startups:

* Pre-seed to Series A
* 1–10 employees
* Founder-led companies

---

## Buyer Personas

### Builder Founder

Characteristics:

* Non-technical or semi-technical founder
* Needs MVP built quickly
* Needs technical partner
* Under pressure to show traction

Buying Triggers:

* MVP development
* Product validation
* Replace manual workflows
* Build SaaS product

---

### Overloaded Technical Founder

Characteristics:

* Founder writing most code
* Needs capacity
* Needs architecture help

Buying Triggers:

* Refactor prototype
* Extend engineering team
* Ship faster
* Add AI or integrations

---

# Business Goals

Primary Goal:

Increase **qualified founder meetings**

Secondary Goals:

* Improve lead quality
* Reduce low-fit leads
* Improve conversion rate
